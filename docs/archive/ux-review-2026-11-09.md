# UX & copy review – 9 Nov 2026

Review of the running app (13.03.1991, several channels) and of all UI text in `apps/web`. Decisions marked **(decided)** come from the owner; everything else is a recommendation to implement. UI text stays German; code, comments and commits stay English (see CLAUDE.md).

## 1. Rename the product (decided)

New name: **„Zappen durch die Zeit“**. The old name „Was lief damals?“ becomes the tagline/claim (it is the question people actually search for).

- Header `<h1>` in `components/TvApp.tsx`: `Zappen durch die Zeit · {Datum}`. On narrow screens the date may wrap below.
- `app/layout.tsx` metadata: title `Zappen durch die Zeit`, description e.g. `Was lief damals? Deutsches Fernsehen wie vor über 30 Jahren – zur gleichen Uhrzeit wie jetzt. Einfach einschalten und durchzappen.`
- Page titles (`info`, `mitmachen`, `admin/beitraege`, `tag/[date]`): suffix `– Zappen durch die Zeit`.
- `components/EmptyDatabase.tsx`, `CLAUDE.md`, `ARCHITECTURE.md` heading.
- Add an Open Graph title/description + share image later (one screenshot of the TV).
- Internal names (`@wld/*` packages, `wld-*` localStorage keys) can stay; renaming them is not worth the churn. If they are renamed, migrate the localStorage keys.

## 2. Station names (decided)

Rule: **each station is shown with the name it carried for most of 1990–2000**, on every day (no per-era names). Hence `RTL` (not „RTL plus“) and `SAT.1` (not „SAT 1“) stay.

Bug found: the channel list on 13.03.1991 shows **„PRO 7“**, although `packages/core/src/stations.ts` says `ProSieben`. The displayed name comes from the day's seed/source data, not from the station registry.
- Always take the display name from `STATIONS` (by slug); keep the source's spelling only in `raw`/import data.
- Apply the rule to the whole registry and verify the dates: e.g. `ProSieben` (renamed from PRO 7 in the mid-90s) ✔, `Kabel 1` → probably `kabel eins` (name since ~1995 – verify), `RTL II` (verify against the 1990–2000 majority rule), `N3`, `WDR Fernsehen`, `MDR`.
- Add a unit test: every channel name rendered for a day equals the registry name.

## 3. First screen (power screen)

Problem: a first-time visitor sees two buttons and 4 lines of privacy text, but nothing explains the idea. The core promise („läuft gerade, zur gleichen Uhrzeit wie damals“) only appears in small print under the Live-Modus checkbox.

- Add one line above the buttons, e.g. `Mittwoch, 13. März 1991 – es läuft, was damals um 17:38 Uhr lief.` (live clock).
- Shorten the privacy text to one sentence + link: `Videos kommen von YouTube, Dailymotion, Internet Archive und Vimeo – dabei werden Daten an diese Anbieter übertragen. Mehr unter Info.`
- The same privacy paragraph is repeated in `ConsentOverlay` (Screen.tsx). Use the short version there too.

## 4. „Ohne Videos einschalten“ looks broken

After choosing „Ohne Videos einschalten“, every channel shows the consent overlay on top of the programme card; title and description shine through the buttons (overlapping text). It reappears on every channel switch.

Either remove this option, or make the no-video mode clean: show the full programme card only, plus a small, non-overlapping link `Videos aktivieren`. Never overlay buttons on top of card text.

## 5. „Live-Modus“ checkbox → two-state switch

The label is misleading (nothing is live) and inverted (checked = you can *not* pick shows). The hint `an – es läuft, was damals jetzt lief` is awkward German.

- Replace with a segmented switch: **„Wie damals“** | **„Programm frei wählen“**.
- Hint for „Wie damals“: `Es läuft, was am 13.3.1991 um diese Uhrzeit lief.` For „frei wählen“: `Klicke im Programm auf eine Sendung.`
- Update the Info page text („Ohne ‚Live-Modus‘ …“) accordingly.

## 6. Channel list: two kinds of grey

On 1991 days 8 of 14 slots are grey, and two different cases look identical:
- station did not exist yet (RTL II, VOX, Super RTL …) → keep grey/snow, `gab es noch nicht`
- station existed but we have no schedule (3sat, N3, WDR in 1991) → different style (e.g. normal colour, dimmed, with a small „?“), screen text `Für 3sat fehlt uns das Programm dieses Tages.` + link `Programm beisteuern` (→ /mitmachen prefilled).

Also: „WDR Fernse…“ is truncated → use a short label (`WDR`) in the list, full name in the OSD/teletext.

## 7. Match-level marks

- `🔵 Ähnliches` is too vague → `Andere Folge / ähnliches Video`; short form in the teletext legend `ähnlich` is fine.
- Colour scale: green → yellow → **orange** → **grey** reads as a quality scale; blue breaks it and red ❌ looks like an error. Suggest 🟢 genau · 🟡 gleiches Jahr · 🟠 ähnlich · ⚪/grey `kein Video`.
- Inside the teletext, emoji break the look. Render the marks as coloured teletext blocks (■) in the teletext palette; keep emoji/badges elsewhere if wanted.
- Update `lib/labels.ts`, CSS classes `b-*`/`m-*`, the Info page legend, the coverage tooltip, and the colour note in CLAUDE.md together.

## 8. Wording table

| Where | Now | New | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| header chip | `Videos 27 %` | `27 % mit Video` | reads like a loading bar |
| chip tooltip | `Punkte je Sendung (genau 5, …)` | `Anteil der Sendungen dieses Tages mit Video (genaue Treffer zählen mehr).` | internal formula is jargon |
| TV panel | `Filter: an` | `Röhre: an` | „Filter“ says nothing |
| TV panel | `GRUNDIG-ish · STEREO` | invented brand, e.g. `ZEITFUNK · STEREO` | Grundig is a real trademark |
| programme card | `Bei YouTube öffnen` / `In der ZDF Mediathek` | `Bei YouTube ansehen` / `In der ZDF-Mediathek ansehen` | one pattern; hyphen „ZDF-Mediathek“ |
| consent overlay | `Beim Anbieter öffnen` | `Beim Anbieter ansehen` | same pattern |
| contribution form | `Bezug:` | `Es geht um:` | friendlier |
| programme card | `Noch kein Video zugeordnet.` | `Zu dieser Sendung haben wir noch kein Video.` | plainer |
| info page | `an einem Tag der 90er-Jahre` | `an einem Tag zwischen 1990 und 1999` (or whatever the data covers) | must match the timeline |

## 9. Year timeline

2000–2005 are shown but have no days. Hide years without data, or render them clearly as `bald` (not clickable). Info text must match what is shown.

„Ersatzprogramm“ (authentic period term), „JETZT“, „Danach: Sendeschluss“, „SENDEPAUSE“ + „Programmbeginn …“ on the test card, teletext page numbers P301…, `Programm: HÖRZU 10/1991` in the header (credibility), the random-day dice, the „gab es noch nicht“ off-air cards, and the warm du-tone of /mitmachen.
